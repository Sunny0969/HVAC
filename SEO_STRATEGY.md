## 5. Competitors' Target Keywords Reviewed
We reviewed top competitors in the Florida business brokerage space. 
- **Competitor A (General Florida Brokers):** Targets generic terms like "Florida business broker" missing the HVAC niche.
- **Competitor B (National HVAC Brokers):** Targets "HVAC business broker" globally, lacking Florida-specific local SEO.
- **Our Strategy:** Hyper-focus on the intersection of **HVAC + Florida**. This gives us a strong competitive edge in local SEO and niche authority.

## 6. Implementation in Code
- Replaced generic Next.js keywords in `src/app/layout.tsx` with targeted business keywords.
- Optimized Meta Titles and Descriptions across core pages (Home, Sell, Buy, Valuation).
- Ensured Search Intent accurately aligns with the page content and Call-to-Actions.
